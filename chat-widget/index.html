Support chat demo

This page embeds widget.js, the chat bubble in the bottom-right corner. Messages go to the n8n workflow, where Claude drafts an answer, Gemini checks it, and anything uncertain goes to a human in Slack.

1. Point the widget at your n8n

Paste your production webhook URL. It is saved in this browser only. For your own site, set WEBHOOK_URL at the top of widget.js instead.

2. Simulate a signed-in customer

On a real site your login backend writes a row to Turso session_tokens and passes the token to the widget. Here you can paste one to test order lookups.

3. Try these messages

Click one to open the chat with it prefilled.

Embed it on any site

One script tag. Optional attributes override the defaults in widget.js.

<script src="https://YOUR-USER.github.io/YOUR-REPO/chat-widget/widget.js"
        data-webhook-url="https://n8n.example.com/webhook/support-chat"
        data-title="Northwind Goods support"
        data-accent="#0b6e4f"
        defer></script>

Signed-in pages can add data-session-token, data-customer-email and data-customer-name, or call SupportChat.identify({ token, email, name }).

Demo store. Product names, orders and people are fictional.
